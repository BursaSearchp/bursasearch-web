Which UK universities give the biggest bursaries?

University of Oxford gives the biggest: up to £6,270 a year (Crankstart Bursary, household income up to £7,500), then Imperial College London (£5,000) and LSE (£4,250). These are each university's main income-based bursary for 2026/27: money you don't pay back.

Questions

Which UK university gives the biggest bursary?

Of the 30 universities here, University of Oxford gives the most: up to £6,270 a year through the Crankstart Bursary for household incomes up to £7,500.

Do you have to pay a bursary back?

No. A university bursary is money you don't repay, unlike the student loan.

What household income do I need?

Each university sets its own limit. Here they range from £10,000 to £35,000 a year, and many pay a smaller amount as income rises. The table shows each limit.

How do I get one?

Most of these bursaries use the household income that Student Finance assesses, so you usually need to apply for student finance and agree to share your income details with your university. Check each university's page for its exact rules.
