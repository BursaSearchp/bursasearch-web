Common questions

Is this list free to use?

Yes. Every bursary listed here for law students is free to browse — no account or payment needed to see what's available.

Do I apply through FindMyFund?

No — every listing links directly to the official university or provider page, and you apply there. We connect you straight to the source, not through a form with us.

How do I know which ones I actually qualify for?

This page lists what's publicly available for law students. FindMyFund's app checks your specific circumstances — income, region, fee status and more — against our full database, which also includes national and independent grants beyond this list, and tells you exactly which ones you qualify for and why.
