Questions

What is the biggest undergraduate bursary at Queen's University Belfast?

The International Scholarships (general, QUB), worth £7,500 (international students).

Does Queen's University Belfast have a hardship fund?

Yes. Queen's University Belfast lists Student Support Fund (QUB) and University Financial Assistance Fund (QUB). These help if you run short of money during your course.

What does Queen's University Belfast offer care leavers and estranged students?

Care Experienced Bursary (£1,000); Queen's Care Leavers Bursary (£1,000).

Is there funding for master's and PhD students at Queen's University Belfast?

Yes, 17 postgraduate funds, including Fulbright-QUB Award in Conflict, Security and Contested Pasts and Fulbright-Queen's University Belfast Award.
