Questions

Do I need to apply for the Durham Undergraduate Bursary?

No. The Durham Undergraduate Bursary is paid automatically, based on the household income you give Student Finance. Check the official page for the income bands.

What is the biggest undergraduate bursary at Durham University?

The Durham University Military Scholarship, worth £20,000 (stem).

Does Durham University have a hardship fund?

Yes. Durham University lists Student Support Fund. These help if you run short of money during your course.

What does Durham University offer care leavers and estranged students?

Bell Scholarship (University College) (£4,000); Dunkley Scholarship (University College) (£5,000); Hogan Lovells Cadwalader Scholarship (£9,000).

Is there funding for master's and PhD students at Durham University?

Yes, 46 postgraduate funds, including ESBF Scholarships and MBA Scholarships for Scientists (ESBF Scholarships).
