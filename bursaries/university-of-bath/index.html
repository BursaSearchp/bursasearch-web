Questions

What is the biggest undergraduate bursary at University of Bath?

The Amazon Future Engineer Bursary, worth £20,000 (first-years).

Does University of Bath have a hardship fund?

Yes. University of Bath lists Financial Support Fund and University of Bath Financial Support Fund. These help if you run short of money during your course.

What does University of Bath offer care leavers and estranged students?

Alumni Fund Placement Grant (£2,000); Care Experienced/Estranged Student Welcome Pack; Care Leaver, Estranged or Refugee Bursary (£1,000).

Is there funding for master's and PhD students at University of Bath?

Yes, 10 postgraduate funds, including Bath Merit Scholarship and GREAT Scholarships (Bath).
