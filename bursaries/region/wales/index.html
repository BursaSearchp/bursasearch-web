Common questions

Is this list free to use?

Yes. Every bursary listed here for students in Wales is free to browse — no account or payment needed to see what's available.

No — every listing links directly to the official university or provider page, and you apply there. We connect you straight to the source, not through a form with us.

This page lists what's publicly available for students in Wales.
