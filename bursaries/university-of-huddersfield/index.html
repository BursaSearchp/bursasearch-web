Questions

What is the biggest undergraduate bursary at University of Huddersfield?

The Merit-Based Scholarships (Huddersfield), worth £4,000 (international students).

Does University of Huddersfield have a hardship fund?

Yes. University of Huddersfield lists NHS Learning Support Fund (Huddersfield) and University Hardship Fund (Huddersfield). These help if you run short of money during your course.

What does University of Huddersfield offer care leavers and estranged students?

Care Experienced Bursary (Huddersfield) (£2,500-£3,250); Care Leaver Bursary (Huddersfield) (£2,000); Care Leaver's Support (University Bursary, Huddersfield) (£2,000).

Is there funding for master's and PhD students at University of Huddersfield?

Yes, 38 postgraduate funds, including PhD Studentship: Cognitive Effects of E-Cigarettes and PhD Studentship: Improving Mounted Police PPE.
