    Questions

    What's the difference between a scholarship and a bursary?

    A bursary is usually based on need, like household income, and is often paid automatically. A scholarship usually rewards grades, a talent or a subject and you normally apply for it. Neither is paid back.

    Do you have to pay a scholarship back?

    No. Scholarships are not loans.

    Can you get a scholarship and a bursary at the same time?

    Often yes. Many universities let you hold an income-based bursary and a scholarship together. Each fund's own rules say whether it can be combined.
