Questions

Do I need to apply for the University of Surrey Bursary?

No. The University of Surrey Bursary is paid automatically, based on the household income you give Student Finance. Check the official page for the income bands.

What is the biggest undergraduate bursary at University of Surrey?

The The Sky Vet School Scholarship, worth £20,000 (care-experienced and others).

Does University of Surrey have a hardship fund?

Yes. University of Surrey lists Hardship Fund (Surrey). These help if you run short of money during your course.

What does University of Surrey offer care leavers and estranged students?

Battersea Scholarship (£4,000); Care Leaver and Estranged Student Bursary (£3,000); Fluor Scholarship (£4,000).

Is there funding for master's and PhD students at University of Surrey?

Yes, 18 postgraduate funds, including Investigating the role of pericytes in exercise-induced pulmonary haemorrhage disease and progression and Resilient digitalised critical infrastructure.
