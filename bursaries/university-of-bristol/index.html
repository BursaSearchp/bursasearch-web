Questions

What is the biggest undergraduate bursary at University of Bristol?

The Futures Scholarships, worth £4,500.

Does University of Bristol have a hardship fund?

Yes. University of Bristol lists Bristol SU's Activity Hardship Fund, Cash Award (Financial Assistance Fund, Bristol) and Food Voucher (Financial Assistance Fund, Bristol). These help if you run short of money during your course.

What does University of Bristol offer care leavers and estranged students?

Cash Award (Financial Assistance Fund, Bristol) (£300-£900).

Is there funding for master's and PhD students at University of Bristol?

Yes, 13 postgraduate funds, including Bristol PhD Scholarships for Black and Mixed Black Heritage Applicants and Bristol PhD Scholarship for Black and Mixed Black Heritage (ALSS).
