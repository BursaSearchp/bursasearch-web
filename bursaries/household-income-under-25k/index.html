Questions

Can I get a bursary if my family earns under £25,000?

At most universities, yes. 21 of the 30 universities on this page pay their main bursary to students from households earning under £25,000.

How much could I get?

Up to £4,250 a year at LSE. Most pay £1,000 to £3,500 a year.

Is household income before or after tax?

Student Finance looks at your household's income before tax, with some allowances such as pension contributions, and universities usually use that same figure. Check the university's page for its exact rules.

Do I need to apply separately?

Often not: many universities pay their bursary automatically using your Student Finance income assessment, as long as you agree to share it. Some need a separate form, so check each page.
