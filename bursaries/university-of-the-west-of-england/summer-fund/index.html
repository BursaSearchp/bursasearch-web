Summer Fund

University of the West of England · Bursary

The Summer Fund is a bursary for care-experienced students at University of the West of England.

Who can apply

  • You are any one of: care leaver, carer (adult), young carer, estranged or disabled / long-term health condition.
  • Your fee status is UK Domiciled.
  • You are ordinarily resident in United Kingdom.
  • You are studying at undergraduate, postgraduate level.

Common questions

How much is the Summer Fund?

University of the West of England doesn't publish a single fixed figure for the Summer Fund — check the official page for the current amount and how it's paid.

What is the deadline for the Summer Fund?

University of the West of England sets the deadline for the Summer Fund each year. Check the official page for the current closing date.

Do I apply through BursaSearch?

No. You apply directly with University of the West of England using the official link on this page — BursaSearch is not part of the application.
