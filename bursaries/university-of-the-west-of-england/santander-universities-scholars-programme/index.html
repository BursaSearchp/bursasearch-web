Santander Universities Scholars Programme

University of the West of England · Bursary

The Santander Universities Scholars Programme is a bursary worth £30,000 for care-experienced students at University of the West of England. Every detail here is taken from the official page — you apply directly with University of the West of England, not through us.

Amount
£30,000
Type
Bursary
Deadline
Set annually — check the official page

Undergraduate
Fee status
UK Domiciled
Administered by
University of the West of England

Who can apply

  • Your circumstances include: low income, polar (low participation area), disabled / long-term health condition, care leaver, estranged, carer (adult).
  • Your fee status is UK Domiciled.
  • You are ordinarily resident in United Kingdom.
  • You are studying at undergraduate level.
