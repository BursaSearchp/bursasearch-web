    Welsh Government Learning Grant (Wales)

    Who: Students from Wales on full-time or part-time undergraduate courses.

    A grant towards living costs that you don't pay back. The amount depends on household income. Apply through Student Finance Wales.

    Official page →

    Disabled Students' Allowance (UK)

    Who: Undergraduates and postgraduates with a disability, long-term illness, mental health condition or a specific learning difficulty such as dyslexia.

    Pays the study costs your condition causes: specialist equipment, non-medical helpers like note takers, and taxis if you can't use public transport. Based on your needs, not household income, and never paid back. Apply through your student finance body; a needs assessment decides what you get.
