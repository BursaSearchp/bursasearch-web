    Who: Students from Wales on full-time or part-time undergraduate courses.

    Who: Scottish students under 25 when their course starts, from lower-income households.

    Not paid back; the amount depends on household income. Students 25 or over, or who support themselves, can get the Independent Students' Bursary instead. Apply through SAAS.
