Questions

What is the biggest undergraduate bursary at External / National Bodies?

The RAEng Lord Bhattacharyya Higher Education Bursaries, worth £15,000 (first-years).

Does External / National Bodies have a hardship fund?

Yes. External / National Bodies lists IMechE Student Grants (hardship) and RCN Foundation Hardship Grants. These help if you run short of money during your course.

What does External / National Bodies offer care leavers and estranged students?

Local Authority Higher Education Bursary for Care Leavers (£2,000); The Leathersellers' Company Undergraduate Student Grants (Up to £5,000 a year).

Is there funding for master's and PhD students at External / National Bodies?

Yes, 24 postgraduate funds, including RAEng Sainsbury Management Fellowships and Gray's Inn Bar Course Scholarships.
