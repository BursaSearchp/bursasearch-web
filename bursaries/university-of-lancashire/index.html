Questions

What is the biggest undergraduate bursary at University of Lancashire?

The Training Grant (NHS LSF, UCLan), worth £5,000.

Does University of Lancashire have a hardship fund?

Yes. University of Lancashire lists The Medivet Hardship Fund. These help if you run short of money during your course.

What does University of Lancashire offer care leavers and estranged students?

Care Leaver Bursary (UCLan); Care-experienced and Estranged Student Graduation Bursary; Degree Apprentice Care Leaver Bursary (£3,000).

Is there funding for master's and PhD students at University of Lancashire?

Yes, 3 postgraduate funds, including Journalism Innovation and Leadership PGCert Scholarship and Alumni Loyalty Discount (UCLan).
