How much does uni cost a month?

University of Edinburgh and Heriot-Watt University are the most expensive at £1,350 a month (£823 rent and bills plus £527 everyday spending). The cheapest here is Swansea University at £1,079. The average maintenance loan is £640 a month, so every one of these costs more than the loan covers.

Questions

How much does it cost to live at university a month?

The average student spends £1,142 a month (Save the Student National Student Money Survey 2025). By university it ranges from £1,079 at Swansea University to £1,350 at University of Edinburgh, using local rent and bills.

Does the student loan cover living costs?

Usually not on its own. The average maintenance loan is £640 a month, which is less than the monthly cost at every university here. Bursaries, grants and part-time work fill the gap.

What's included in these figures?

Average rent and bills for a student room near each university (SpareRoom university rent index, Q2 2025 (rent incl. bills, asking prices)) plus £527 a month for food, travel, going out and everything else (Save the Student National Student Money Survey 2025).

How can I cover the gap?

Check the bursaries and grants your university and others offer: many are paid automatically if your household income is below a limit, and you don't pay them back.
