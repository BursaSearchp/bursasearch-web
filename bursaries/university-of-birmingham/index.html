Questions

Do I need to apply for the Chamberlain Award?

No. The Chamberlain Award is paid automatically, based on the household income you give Student Finance. Check the official page for the income bands.

What is the biggest undergraduate bursary at University of Birmingham?

The High Fliers Undergraduate Scholarships for Turkey, worth £5,000-£10,000 (international students).

Does University of Birmingham have a hardship fund?

Yes. University of Birmingham lists Student Support Fund (Birmingham). These help if you run short of money during your course.

What does University of Birmingham offer care leavers and estranged students?

Care Leaver Scholarship (£2,000); Care-Experienced and Estranged Student Independence Fund (£500); Enhanced Chamberlain Award (£3,500-£9,848).

Is there funding for master's and PhD students at University of Birmingham?

Yes, 40 postgraduate funds, including Birmingham Business School Masters Scholarships and MBA Scholarship (Birmingham Business School).
