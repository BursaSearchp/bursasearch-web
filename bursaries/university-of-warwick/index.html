Questions

Do I need to apply for the Warwick Undergraduate Bursary?

No. The Warwick Undergraduate Bursary is paid automatically, based on the household income you give Student Finance. Check the official page for the income bands.

What is the biggest undergraduate bursary at University of Warwick?

The Qube Scholarship for Women in Computing, worth £15,000 (computer science).

Does University of Warwick have a hardship fund?

Yes. University of Warwick lists Diagnostic Test Reimbursement (Warwick Hardship Fund), International Students Emergency Fund (ISEF) and PGR Hardship Support Fund (Doctoral College). These help if you run short of money during your course.

What does University of Warwick offer care leavers and estranged students?

Warwick Taught Masters Scholarship Scheme (£10,000); Widening Participation Bursary for Independent Students who are Care Leavers or Estranged (£1,500).

Is there funding for master's and PhD students at University of Warwick?

Yes, 85 postgraduate funds, including Chemistry Overseas Taught Masters Scholarship (Warwick) and GREAT Scholarships (Warwick).
