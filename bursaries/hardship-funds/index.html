    Questions

    What is a university hardship fund?

    Money a university sets aside for students who run into money problems during their course, for example with rent, bills or an unexpected cost. Most give grants you don't pay back; a few also offer short-term loans, so check the official page.

    Who can apply?

    Usually students who are already enrolled and have taken up their full student finance but still can't cover essential costs. Each fund sets its own rules, so check the official page before applying.

    How much can you get across 66 universities?

    The largest listed across 66 universities is the International Students' Emergency Fund (Edinburgh) (University of Edinburgh), worth £12,346. Most awards depend on your situation.

    When can I apply?

    The next fixed deadline is the Digital Support Fund (Reading) on 29 January 2027. Hardship money can run out before the end of the year, so apply as soon as you need it.
