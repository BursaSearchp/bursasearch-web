Questions

Do I need to apply for the The Manchester Bursary?

No. The The Manchester Bursary is paid automatically, based on the household income you give Student Finance. Check the official page for the income bands.

What is the biggest undergraduate bursary at University of Manchester?

The Sport Scholarships (Manchester, International), worth £9,000 (sport · international students).

Does University of Manchester have a hardship fund?

Yes. University of Manchester lists Access to Learning Fund (Manchester) and Cost of Living Fund (Manchester). These help if you run short of money during your course.

What does University of Manchester offer care leavers and estranged students?

Care Experienced and Estranged Student Bursary (Manchester) (£1,300); Sadler Bursary (£1,000); The Bason Family Bursary (£6,000).

Is there funding for master's and PhD students at University of Manchester?

Yes, 48 postgraduate funds, including Kennedy Trust IMPACT Inflammation MB-PhD and CRUK Manchester Institute Studentships.
